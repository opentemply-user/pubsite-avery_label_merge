Use Cases for Amazon FBA Labels

  • FNSKU labels: Product identification barcodes for FBA inventory. Amazon's unique barcode per ASIN — required on every unit.
  • Shipping labels: 4×6 shipping labels for FBA inbound shipments. One label per box in your shipment.
  • Box content labels: Labels identifying the contents of each carton sent to Amazon fulfilment centres.
  • Mixed SKU labels: When one carton contains multiple products — label with carton ID and contents list.
  • Suffocation warnings: "WARNING: To avoid danger of suffocation, keep this bag away from babies and children." Required for poly-bagged items. Handled securely and privately by OpenTemply.
  • Oversize labels: Special labels for oversize and heavy/bulky items with handling instructions.

Recommended Label Sizes

  • 57 × 32 mm (DYMO 11354): FNSKU labels (standard)
  • 89 × 36 mm (DYMO 99012): FNSKU with product name
  • 101 × 152 mm (4×6 Shipping): FBA shipping labels
  • 1 × 2⅝ in (30/Letter) (Avery 5160): FNSKU on sheets
