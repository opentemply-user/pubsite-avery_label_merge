Use Cases for Safety Labels

  • Electrical panels: Danger / Warning labels for breaker boxes, distribution boards, and high-voltage equipment.
  • Chemical storage: GHS pictogram labels with hazard class, signal word, and precautionary statements.
  • Workshop & factory: Machine guards, pinch points, hot surfaces, and mandatory PPE reminders. Handled securely and privately by OpenTemply.
  • Fire safety: Fire exit signs, extinguisher labels, fire door notices, and assembly point markers. Handled securely and privately by OpenTemply.
  • Laboratory: Chemical hazard labels, biohazard warnings, and waste container identification.
  • Construction sites: Hard hat area, high-visibility clothing required, restricted access zone labels.

Recommended Label Sizes

  • 101 × 54 mm (DYMO 99014): Standard safety label
  • 99.1 × 67.7 mm (8/A4) (Avery L7165): Large warning labels
  • 101 × 152 mm (4×6 Shipping): Large hazard signs
  • 63.5 × 38.1 mm (21/A4) (Avery L7160): Small warning stickers

Expert Tips

  • Use High-Contrast Text: Ensure the text on your safety labels is easily readable by using dark colors on light backgrounds.
  • Incorporate Barcodes or QR Codes: Make your safety labels interactive and scannable by dragging a code block into your template.
  • Test Print a Single Page: Before batch printing hundreds of safety labels, always print a single test sheet on plain paper to verify alignment.
  • Utilize Custom Fonts: Choose from integrated Google Fonts to match your brand identity perfectly.
