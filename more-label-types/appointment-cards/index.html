Batch Print from a Spreadsheet

Have a list of patients with upcoming appointments? Upload your CSV or Excel file with Name, Date, Time, and Doctor fields. OpenTemply Label Maker prints one card per appointment — with all details filled in automatically.

Expert Tips

  • Use High-Contrast Text: Ensure the text on your appointment cards is easily readable by using dark colors on light backgrounds.
  • Incorporate Barcodes or QR Codes: Make your appointment cards interactive and scannable by dragging a code block into your template.
  • Test Print a Single Page: Before batch printing hundreds of appointment cards, always print a single test sheet on plain paper to verify alignment.
  • Utilize Custom Fonts: Choose from integrated Google Fonts to match your brand identity perfectly.
