Recommended Label Sizes

  • 99.1 × 38.1 mm (14/A4) (Avery L7163): Standard bin labels
  • 99.1 × 67.7 mm (8/A4) (Avery L7165): Large box labels
  • 101 × 54 mm (DYMO 99014): Thermal bin labels
  • 89 × 36 mm (DYMO 99012): Drawer & basket labels

Expert Tips

  • Use High-Contrast Text: Ensure the text on your storage labels is easily readable by using dark colors on light backgrounds.
  • Incorporate Barcodes or QR Codes: Make your storage labels interactive and scannable by dragging a code block into your template.
  • Test Print a Single Page: Before batch printing hundreds of storage labels, always print a single test sheet on plain paper to verify alignment.
  • Utilize Custom Fonts: Choose from integrated Google Fonts to match your brand identity perfectly.
