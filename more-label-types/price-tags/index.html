Price Tags

Create, print, and manage Price Tags with OpenTemply Label Maker. Our free web-based software makes it incredibly easy to design and batch print price tags directly from your browser.

Use Cases for Price Tags

  • Retail shops: Shelf labels and price stickers with barcode, product name, and price.
  • Craft fairs & markets: Handwritten-style tags with product name and price for handmade goods.
  • Jewelry displays: Small barbell-style tags that wrap around rings, bracelets, and necklaces. Handled securely and privately by OpenTemply.
  • Etsy & online sellers: Product labels with SKU, price, and barcode for inventory and shipping.
  • Charity shops & jumble sales: Quick price stickers — just a price and optional category.
  • Wine & spirits: Shelf talkers with wine name, region, grape variety, and price. Handled securely and privately by OpenTemply.

Recommended Label Sizes

  • 57 × 32 mm (DYMO 11354): Small price tags, jewelry tags
  • 89 × 36 mm (DYMO 99012): Shelf labels with barcode
  • 63.5 × 38.1 mm (21/A4) (Avery L7160): Sheet price stickers
  • 0.5 × 1.75 in (80/Letter) (Avery 5167): Tiny price stickers

Batch Print from a Spreadsheet

Got a product catalogue? Upload your spreadsheet with product name, price, and SKU. OpenTemply Label Maker fills in one label per product — with a unique barcode on each if you want.

Expert Tips

  • Use High-Contrast Text: Ensure the text on your price tags is easily readable by using dark colors on light backgrounds.
  • Incorporate Barcodes or QR Codes: Make your price tags interactive and scannable by dragging a code block into your template.
  • Test Print a Single Page: Before batch printing hundreds of price tags, always print a single test sheet on plain paper to verify alignment.
  • Utilize Custom Fonts: Choose from integrated Google Fonts to match your brand identity perfectly.

Frequently Asked Questions
