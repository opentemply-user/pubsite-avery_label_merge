Expert Tips

  • Use High-Contrast Text: Ensure the text on your jar labels is easily readable by using dark colors on light backgrounds.
  • Incorporate Barcodes or QR Codes: Make your jar labels interactive and scannable by dragging a code block into your template.
  • Test Print a Single Page: Before batch printing hundreds of jar labels, always print a single test sheet on plain paper to verify alignment.
  • Utilize Custom Fonts: Choose from integrated Google Fonts to match your brand identity perfectly.
