Use Cases for Inventory Labels

  • Retail stocktaking: SKU labels with barcode for annual or cycle stock counts. Scan to count, scan to reconcile.
  • Warehouse inventory: Bin labels with product ID, description, and reorder level for inventory management. Handled securely and privately by OpenTemply.
  • Serial number tracking: Unique serial number labels for electronics, equipment, and high-value items. Handled securely and privately by OpenTemply.
  • Work-in-progress (WIP): Job tracking labels with order number, stage, and quantity for manufacturing. Handled securely and privately by OpenTemply.
  • Received goods: Inbound inspection labels with PO number, supplier, date received, and quantity. Handled securely and privately by OpenTemply.
  • Consignment stock: Labels identifying consignment items with owner, product code, and agreed price.

Recommended Label Sizes

  • 57 × 32 mm (DYMO 11354): Small inventory stickers
  • 89 × 36 mm (DYMO 99012): Standard inventory labels
  • 101 × 152 mm (4×6 Shipping): Large inventory tags
  • 63.5 × 38.1 mm (21/A4) (Avery L7160): Sheet inventory labels

Expert Tips

  • Use High-Contrast Text: Ensure the text on your inventory labels is easily readable by using dark colors on light backgrounds.
  • Incorporate Barcodes or QR Codes: Make your inventory labels interactive and scannable by dragging a code block into your template.
  • Test Print a Single Page: Before batch printing hundreds of inventory labels, always print a single test sheet on plain paper to verify alignment.
  • Utilize Custom Fonts: Choose from integrated Google Fonts to match your brand identity perfectly.
